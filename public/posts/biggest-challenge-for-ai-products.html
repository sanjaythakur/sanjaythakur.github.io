AI Products Are Fundamentally Harder Than Non-AI Products

Draft

Let me say this: AI startups are fundamentally harder than a similar startup without AI. And when I say this, I don't mean demos or vibe-coded applications. They may be impressive, but when it comes to building a product that is used by millions of people, it is a different ball game.

Make no mistake, the opportunity size is huge. With the direction we have collectively taken by funding and being involved, it is inevitable that it would completely transform the way we operate. But make no mistake either — it is not easy.

That is still the tip of the iceberg. Other second-order challenges emanate from a lack of system design principles like data privacy, security, reliability, scalability, and more.

My observations have been people underestimating how difficult it is to make AI behave in reasonably predictable and high-quality ways.

Conclusion: AI products are fundamentally harder than non-AI products.
